2. Session Scenario

Jane attended her scheduled 45-minute telehealth session. She reported feeling "overwhelmed" during the past week because of increased responsibilities at work and difficulty balancing work and family responsibilities.

Jane reported that she has been sleeping approximately 5–6 hours per night and has noticed difficulty concentrating at work. She stated that she has been using deep breathing techniques discussed during the previous session and found them somewhat helpful.

During the session, the social worker explored Jane's current stressors and discussed time-management strategies. The social worker also reviewed coping skills and encouraged Jane to continue practicing deep breathing exercises.

Jane was engaged throughout the session and demonstrated an understanding of the strategies discussed. She agreed to identify two manageable tasks each day and practice deep breathing at least once daily before the next session.

3. Your Assignment

Using the information provided above, write a professional progress note.

Remember:

  • Document objectively and professionally.
  • Separate client statements from your observations.
  • Focus on clinically relevant information.
  • Avoid unnecessary personal details.
  • Document interventions provided.
  • Document the client's response to the intervention.
  • Include the plan or next steps.
  • Do not add information that is not included in the scenario.

⚠️ Practice exercise: Do not include identifying information about real clients in this activity.
